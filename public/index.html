ABOUT FORBIDDEN SUYA

We Craft Daring Suya, Sizzling Cuts & Feasts That Truly Make Noise

(because regular roadside suya is boooring!)

At Forbidden Suya, we believe extraordinary beef isn’t merely cooked—it’s forged in smoke and flame. Sourced from single-origin heritage cattle and dry-aged for 45 days in our Himalayan pink salt lockers, every bone-in cut is blistered over white oak embers and binchotan charcoal for an unapologetic, mahogany crust and butter-soft marbling.

See How We Turn Embers into OBSESSION

We don’t believe in roadside compromises, gristly cuts, or lukewarm skewers drowned in MSG. We believe in spitting mangrove embers, the intoxicating fragrance of stoneground Northern yaji, sizzling ram tozo fat caramelizing over iron grates, and knives slicing through flame-blistered beef that melts on contact.

The Ram Tozo Ritual

04 / PROVENANCE & SAVANNAH TERROIRS

Our Origins

REGION 01 · SOKOTO & KANO SAVANNAH SAHEL GRASSLANDS · FREE-GRAZING MARBLED TOZO FAT-CAP
Northern Savannah Genetics

Heritage Zebu & Ram Tozo

Pasture-grazed across the vast Sahelian grasslands of Northern Nigeria. Naturally lean muscle encased in rich, heavily marbled hump fat (Tozo). Flashed over 1,000°F mangrove coals, yielding an explosive beef-forward profile with buttery, caramelized crackling edges.
